Submit an enquiry (this form or a call), we will schedule a visit, you complete the registration form and pay the registration fee, submit documents (birth certificate, vaccination record, photos), and we send your Welcome Kit. The whole process can be done in 1–2 weeks.

Yes! We strongly encourage a short orientation session (1–2 hours) before your child's first full day. It helps children familiarise themselves with the space, the teachers, and the rhythm, and it dramatically reduces first-day anxiety for both children and parents.

Tiny Terra Academy follows the NEP 2020 Early Childhood Education framework and is registered as a preschool under state education guidelines. We are not affiliated to any primary school board (CBSE/ICSE) as a preschool does not require such affiliation.

We currently do not operate a school bus. We recommend car-pooling with nearby families (we can help connect you!) or using a trusted cab service. Our location is accessible from the main road and has safe drop-off space. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.
